## Runtime and libraries

- The public packages include Eclipse Temurin OpenJDK 25. Its license files are in the runtime's legal directory. [Temurin source and releases](https://github.com/adoptium/temurin25-binaries)
- JTattoo 1.6.13 supplies all nine historical Swing themes and is used under its Apache license option. [JTattoo](https://jtattoo.de/)
- JavaCPP presets package Tesseract and Leptonica. Both language models come from tessdata_fast at revision 87416418657359cb625c412a48b6e1d6d41c29bd. [Presets and licenses](https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp-presets) · [Models and Apache license](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast)
- Apache PDFBox, POI and Apache Commons supply export and utility code. Noto Sans is embedded in exported PDFs and its SIL Open Font License accompanies the font. [Noto Sans](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts)
- SQLite JDBC provides the embedded database. MariaDB Connector/J supports existing MySQL servers under LGPL-2.1-or-later. The unmodified driver is a separate replaceable JAR. [SQLite JDBC](https://github.com/xerial/sqlite-jdbc) · [Connector/J source](https://github.com/mariadb-corporation/mariadb-connector-j/tree/3.5.7)
- ZXing, Jakarta Mail, Jakarta Activation, SLF4J and their dependencies retain their respective notices in the library JARs. Gradle lists exact resolved versions with `./gradlew dependencies --configuration runtimeClasspath`.
